# 3D Printing & Fabrication Guide

## Material Specifications
- **Recommended Material:** PETG or Tough PLA+
- **Infill Density:**
  - Structural Mounts / Torso Brackets: 35% - 40% (Gyroid)
  - Finger Phalanges & Gripper Linkages: 60% (Solid Strength)
  - Cosmetic Shells & Head Bezel: 20%
- **Walls / Perimeters:** 4 perimeters for load-bearing brackets
- **Layer Height:** 0.20mm (0.16mm for finger hinges)

## Assembly Hardware
- M3 Threaded Brass Heat-Set Inserts: 30 pcs
- M3 x 8mm, 12mm, 16mm Socket Head Cap Screws
- M2.5 x 10mm Knuckle Pivot Screws with Nylon Locknuts
